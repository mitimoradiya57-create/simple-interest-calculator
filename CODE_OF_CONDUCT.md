# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience, gender, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and polite.
- Welcoming different opinions and ideas.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Helping other community members.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting comments, discrimination, and disrespectful communication.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They may take appropriate corrective action when necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related activities.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed fairly and appropriately.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may take the following actions depending on the situation:

1. Warning the individual.
2. Removing inappropriate comments or content.
3. Temporarily restricting participation.
4. Permanently removing participation privileges in serious cases.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from community standards and best practices for open-source projects.
